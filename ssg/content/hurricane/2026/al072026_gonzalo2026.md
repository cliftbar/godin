## Official Advisory Discussion
At 200 AM CVT (0300 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Gonzalo was
located near latitude 13.2 North, longitude 22.1 West. Gonzalo is
moving toward the north-northwest near 8 mph (13 km/h), and this
motion is expected to continue over the next several days, with a
slower forward speed this weekend. On the forecast track, the center
will pass near or over portions of the Cabo Verde Islands today and
tonight.
 
Maximum sustained winds are near 45 mph (75 km/h) with higher gusts. 
Some strengthening is possible today, with a gradual weakening trend 
forecast to begin this weekend.
 
Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 175 miles (280 km)
from the center.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 1004 mb (29.65 inches).
 
 
HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
Key messages for Gonzalo can be found in the Tropical Cyclone
Discussion under AWIPS header MIATCDAT2 and WMO header WTNT42 KNHC.
 
WIND: Tropical storm conditions are expected to reach the Cabo Verde
Islands within the warning area later this morning.
 
RAINFALL: Tropical Storm Gonzalo is expected to produce rainfall
totals of 1 to 3 inches – 30 to 75 mm — across the eastern portions
of the Cabo Verde Islands on Friday and Saturday with less than 1
inch anticipated for the western side of the island chain. Heavy
rain along the coastal areas of Guinea-Bissau, The Gambia, and 
Senegal will produce totals between 2 to 4 inches – 50 to 100 mm – 
with isolated higher amounts along the immediate coast.
 
SURF: Swells generated by the storm are expected to affect the Cabo
Verde Islands through Saturday. These swells are likely to cause
life-threatening surf and rip current conditions. Please consult
products from your local weather office.
