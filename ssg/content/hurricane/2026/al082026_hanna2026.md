## Official Advisory Discussion
At 300 AM GMT (0300 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Hanna was 
located near latitude 33.7 North, longitude 43.6 West. Hanna is 
moving toward the southeast near 12 mph (19 km/h). This 
motion is forecast to continue with a decrease in forward speed 
through morning, followed by a turn to the east-northeast.

gusts. Gradual weakening is expected during the next couple of days, 
and Hanna is likely to become a post-tropical cyclone tonight or 
Thursday.

from the center.
