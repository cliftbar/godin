## Official Advisory Discussion
At 300 PM GMT (1500 UTC), the center of Post-Tropical Cyclone Hanna
was located near latitude 33.4 North, longitude 43.6 West. The
post-tropical cyclone is moving toward the east near 5 mph (7 km/h). 
A turn toward the east-northeast is expected later today with an 
increase in forward speed on Thursday.
 
Maximum sustained winds are near 35 mph (55 km/h) with higher gusts.
The remnant low should gradually weaken during the next couple of 
days.
 
The estimated minimum central pressure is 1008 mb (29.77 inches).
