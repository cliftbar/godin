## Official Advisory Discussion
At 300 PM GMT (1500 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Fay was
located near latitude 29.8 North, longitude 41.2 West. Fay is moving
toward the west near 6 mph (9 km/h). A generally westward motion
with some decrease in forward speed is expected over the next few
days.

through tonight.  A gradual weakening trend is expected to commence
on Friday.

from the center.
