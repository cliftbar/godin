At 300 PM GMT (1500 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Fay was
located near latitude 30.1 North, longitude 42.5 West. Fay is moving
toward the west near 3 mph (6 km/h).  A gradual turn to the
west-southwest then southwest at a slower forward speed is expected
over the next few of days.
