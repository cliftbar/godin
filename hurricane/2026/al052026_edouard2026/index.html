Official Advisory Discussion

At 1000 PM CDT (0300 UTC), the center of Tropical Depression Edouard was located near latitude 30.5 North, longitude 94.6 West. The depression is moving toward the northwest near 8 mph (13 km/h), and this motion is expected to continue for the next day or so. On the forecast track, the center of Edouard will continue moving inland over southeastern and eastern Texas through Wednesday.

Maximum sustained winds have decreased to near 35 mph (55 km/h) with higher gusts. Continued weakening is expected during the next day or so, and Edouard is forecast to weaken to a tropical depression tonight and dissipate by Wednesday night.

The estimated minimum central pressure is 1002 mb (29.59 inches).

HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND

Key messages for Tropical Storm Edouard can be found in the Tropical Cyclone Discussion under AWIPS header MIATCDAT5 and WMO header WTNT45 KNHC.

WIND: Wind gusts to tropical-storm force may continue near the center of Edouard for the next several hours.

RAINFALL: Edouard is expected to produce rainfall totals of 3 to 7 inches with maximum totals up to 12 inches for parts of the Upper Texas Coast inland through east-central Texas, with potential for 2 to 4 inches in far southwest Louisiana. This rainfall is likely to produce flash flooding, especially in low-lying and urban areas. Significant flash flooding is possible. River flooding may also be possible.

For a complete depiction of forecast rainfall associated with Edouard, please see the National Weather Service Storm Total Rainfall Graphic available at hurricanes.gov/graphics_at5.shtml?rainqpf and the Flash Flood Risk graphic at hurricanes.gov/graphics_at5.shtml?ero.

For a list of rainfall observations (and wind reports) associated with Edouard, see the companion storm summary at WBCSCCNS5 with the WMO header ACUS45 KWBC or at the following link: www.wpc.ncep.noaa.gov/discussions/nfdscc5.html.

TORNADOES: A tornado or two is possible through tonight across parts of southwest Louisiana and southeast Texas.
